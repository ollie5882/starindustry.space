GravityWorks — an in-space production platform.

A dockable, reusable host bus delivering kilowatts of power and a regulated thermal / microgravity environment to customer payloads in orbit. Designed for in-space manufacturing today — with the same platform extending to data-centre, biomedical and semiconductor hosting tomorrow.

Pathfinder → v1 establishes the multi-bay architecture. From there, v2 (high-precision microgravity) and v4 (dedicated data centre) are parallel variants of v1 — v3 is the evolution of v2 to multi-use bays.

Roadmap & Vision

Bootstrapping from composites to in-orbit production.

Each phase enables the next. The composites and digital-engineering capabilities established today provide the architectural baseline for the GravityWorks platform — which in turn scales into a recurring in-orbit production capability.

Horizon 1 · Near-Term
2026 – 2027

Composites manufacturing + digital engineering

  • Stand up UK composites facility (Forres, Moray)
  • First CFRP-tank and COPV deliveries
  • Establish CONVERj as a recurring digital-engineering and consultancy capability
  • Validate the engineering-services engagement model
Horizon 2 · Mid-Term
2027 – 2029

GravityWorks production platform — first flight

  • Close engineering deltas against the platform baseline
  • First-flight hosting service for in-space manufacturing payloads
  • Composites & CONVERj capabilities operating at scale
Horizon 3 · Long-Term
2029 +

Recurring in-orbit production at scale

  • Second-generation GravityWorks platform in service
  • Hosted payload classes expand: data, biomedical, semiconductor
  • Recurring in-orbit manufacturing service
  • European platform infrastructure for the in-space economy
