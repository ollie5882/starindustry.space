How We Scale

A senior team, assembled to fit.

Most engagements are delivered by the principal directly — so you always have a senior engineer, not a junior team under light oversight. When scope grows, a curated network of trusted senior associates is brought in across the disciplines a programme needs: senior-only, independent, no standing overhead, and sized to the work in front of us.

How We Work

Skunkworks by default.

Small, autonomous, time-boxed work that converges on architecture before completing detail. Five beliefs guide how the work gets done.

01
Momentum beats certainty.
Act before certainty; learn through rapid iteration.
02
Architecture before completeness.
Incomplete detail is acceptable; architectural ambiguity is not.
03
Simplicity enables speed.
Complexity is a cost. If two options work, the simpler one wins.
04
Data decides.
Engineering is done with numbers.
05
Ownership is non-delegable.
Every project has one accountable owner and one technical authority.
