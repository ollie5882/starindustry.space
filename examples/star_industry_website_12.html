Tier 3 · Vehicles

Shared bus. Many markets.

Two product families — Industrial Platform and Mobility — share a stack of composites, avionics and propulsion subsystems. Each branch reaches a different market while paying back into a common engineering base.

Industrial Platform

GravityWorks

Production / power platform. Dockable, reusable host bus delivers high peak power across multi-hour cycles — customers swap payload modules between flights; platform amortised across many missions.

Mobility · LEO → GEO

NorthStar

Orbit raising. Modular OTV stages move primes' satellites from rideshare drop-off into GEO, MEO and lunar transfer orbits.

Mobility · Agile

DarkStar

Manoeuvring spacecraft. High-Δv buses for repositionable and life-extended missions — ISR, custody and defence customers.

Mobility · Cislunar

DeepStar

Cislunar logistics. The destination strand — common stack scaled for TLI, recurring delivery service to lunar orbit and surface.

Featured · GravityWorks

An in-space production platform for hosted payloads.

A dockable, reusable host bus delivering kilowatts of power and a regulated thermal / microgravity environment to customer payloads in orbit. Designed for in-space manufacturing today — the same platform extending to data-centre, biomedical and semiconductor hosting tomorrow.

Architecture baseline derived from the Dark Star OTV programme, adapted for multi-tenant production-platform service.

How We Work

Skunkworks by default.

Small, autonomous, time-boxed teams converge on architecture before completing detail. This is the operating norm — not a special exception. As programmes mature, a complementary delivery mode is added deliberately.

Five Core Beliefs
01
Momentum beats certainty.Act before certainty; learn through rapid iteration.
02
Architecture before completeness.Incomplete detail is acceptable; architectural ambiguity is not.
03
Simplicity enables speed.Complexity is a cost. If two options work, the simpler one wins.
04
Data decides.Engineering is done with numbers.
05
Ownership is non-delegable.Every project has one accountable owner and one technical authority.
